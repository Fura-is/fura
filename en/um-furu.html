About Fura

At Fura we want to make recycling simpler, more efficient and accessible for individuals and businesses. Our goal is to improve the environment and ease our customers' workload through personal service, quick responses and reliable solutions.

Over 40 years of experience

Fura has been operating since 1981, and has actively taken part in Icelandic recycling for more than four decades. From the very beginning, the company has evolved alongside growing demands for environmental protection and sustainability. The experience built up over the years enables us to provide safe, professional and efficient service.

What makes Fura different

What sets Fura apart is our experience, our people and our service. Over more than four decades, we have built up expertise and a skilled team that knows the material and the process inside out. We provide personal service and respond quickly. Our goal is always to keep things simple for the customer. We handle the project from start to finish, whether it involves delivering containers, collecting material or assisting with larger projects.
